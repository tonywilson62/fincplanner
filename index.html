    Monte Carlo

    The spread of possible outcomes

    Instead of one smooth return, each year's portfolio return is drawn at random from a bell curve centred on the chosen basis, with the spread set by volatility. Toggle between your base assumptions and the conservative dials from the Stress test (lower return, higher inflation, longer life, plus its one-off shock). Running it many times maps the fan of paths your wealth could take. The shading is densest where outcomes cluster (the middle 50%) and fades through the 80% and 90% ranges at the edges. The dashed line is the smooth, no-volatility projection of the same basis.

    Device sync

    Sync keeps this plan in step across your devices. Your data is encrypted on this device with your passphrase before it is sent, so the server only ever holds unreadable ciphertext. Enter the same passphrase and access token on each device you want in sync.

    The passphrase and token are stored only in this browser. There is no password recovery: if you forget the passphrase, the synced copy cannot be decrypted. Keep a JSON export as a backup.
